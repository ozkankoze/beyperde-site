About

Since 2017, Bey Perde has worked from its workshop in Çekmeköy, bringing curtains, wallpaper and rugs to homes and villas across the north of Istanbul's Asian side — Çekmeköy, Şile, Ağva, Riva, Beykoz and around. For more than nine years we have worked the same way: the right measure, careful sewing, clean fitting.

A curtain is not a decorative detail. It is the part that governs the light in a room.

So light is where we start.

Window, wall and floor: from one house

A room is remembered by the light that falls on it and the surfaces you touch. That is why we think of curtains, wallpaper and rugs together rather than one at a time. From sheers and drapes to motorised systems, from textile wallpaper to made-to-measure rugs, the same team finishes all three soft surfaces of a room with the same care.

How we work

How we work

You choose the fabric in your own light, next to your own furniture. Our workshop sews it and our own team fits it.

  1. 01

    Meeting at our office

    Book an appointment and visit our office in Çekmeköy. We go through fabric books, samples and systems together and talk about what you need face to face.

  2. 02

    Measuring on site

    We come to you and note the measurements, which way the windows face and the light of the day. Our fabric books come with us, so you choose in your own light, next to your own furniture.

  3. 03

    Sewn in our workshop

    The fabric you choose is sewn to your measurements in our own workshop. Orders are ready within 3–8 working days.

  4. 04

    Fitting and care

    Our own team fits rails, poles and motorised systems. Every product carries a 3-year guarantee, and our washing and care support goes on after delivery.

Making one fabric hang the same way across hundreds of windows is harder than getting one living room right — that is where our expertise lies.

Villas and contract work

Alongside homes we take on villas, hotels, offices and large projects. For high ceilings, winter gardens and wide glass façades we install motorised and ceiling curtain systems. We work with interior architects and contractors project by project.
