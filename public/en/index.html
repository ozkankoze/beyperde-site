    How we work

    One team, from measuring to fitting

    You choose the fabric in your own light, next to your own furniture. Our workshop sews it and our own team fits it.

    1. 01

      Meeting at our office

      Book an appointment and visit our office in Çekmeköy. We go through fabric books, samples and systems together and talk about what you need face to face.

    2. 02

      Measuring on site

      We come to you and note the measurements, which way the windows face and the light of the day. Our fabric books come with us, so you choose in your own light, next to your own furniture.

    3. 03

      Sewn in our workshop

      The fabric you choose is sewn to your measurements in our own workshop. Orders are ready within 3–8 working days.

    4. 04

      Fitting and care

      Our own team fits rails, poles and motorised systems. Every product carries a 3-year guarantee, and our washing and care support goes on after delivery.

    How long do orders take?

    Once we have measured and you have chosen the fabric, orders are made in our own workshop within 3–8 working days. Our own team fits them.

    Are your products guaranteed?

    Yes. Every product carries a 3-year guarantee, and our washing and care support goes on after delivery.

    Can you make new curtains for my existing track?

    Yes. At the measuring visit we check the condition of your track and whether it can carry the new curtain. If it can, we make the curtain to suit it.

    Should I choose a Venetian or a roller blind?

    If you want to adjust the light by angle through the day, a Venetian blind is more flexible. If you want a plain single surface of fabric or full blackout, a roller blind may suit you better. We decide together once we have seen the window.

    How many rolls of wallpaper does a room need?

    It depends on the size of the walls, the width and length of the roll and the pattern repeat. We measure in your home and work out the quantity, allowing for waste.

    Which areas do you serve?

    From our workshop in Çekmeköy we serve homes and villas in Çekmeköy, Şile, Ağva, Riva, Beykoz and around.
