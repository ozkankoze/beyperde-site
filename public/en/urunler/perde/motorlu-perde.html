Set the light with a single touch

Motorised curtains — also called electric, remote-controlled or smart curtains — let you open and close sheers, drapes and roller blinds by remote or from your phone. You can let the morning in without getting out of bed, and shade the living room from the midday sun with one touch. They make daily life easier at windows you cannot reach, in double-height rooms and along floor-to-ceiling glazing, and they spare the fabric from being tugged by hand.

How it works

On motorised sheers and drapes the motor sits at the end of the track, behind the curtain; on motorised roller and zebra blinds it is hidden inside the tube the fabric winds onto. The curtain moves at the same speed and along the same clean line every time. You can run the sheer and the drape separately, or open and close several windows at once. When we come to measure, we recommend the system that suits your window, having seen the room.

Where it works best

A motorised curtain makes the most difference where a hand cannot reach: double-height living rooms in villas, winter gardens, attic floors and wide glass façades. We fit these systems in homes and villas across Çekmeköy, Şile, Ağva, Riva and Beykoz, and in hotel and office projects. The motor needs a power point near the window. In a new build or a renovation, planning it from the start gives the cleanest result, with no cable in sight.

Measuring, fitting and guarantee

It begins with a measure taken in your room: together we settle which way the window faces, the ceiling height and where the motor goes. You choose the fabric from our sample books in your own light; your curtain is sewn to size in our own workshop, and orders are ready within 3–8 working days. Our own team fits the track and the motor. Like every product we make, our motorised systems carry a 3-year guarantee, and our washing and care support goes on after delivery.

Can a motor be fitted to my existing curtains?

That depends on your current track and how heavy your curtains are. When we come to measure we look at both and tell you on the spot whether they can be adapted to a motorised system.

Does a motorised curtain need wiring?

Yes, the motor needs a power point near the window. In a new build or a renovation, planning that point in advance keeps every cable out of sight; we settle the right place together when we measure.

How long does a motorised curtain take?

Once the measure is taken and the fabric chosen, orders are ready within 3–8 working days. Our own team fits the track and the motor and hands the system over in working order.

Is there a guarantee on motorised curtains?

Yes. Like every product we make, our motorised systems carry a 3-year guarantee, and our washing and care support goes on after delivery.
