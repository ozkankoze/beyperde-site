The details that carry a curtain

How a curtain falls, how it runs and how it holds up over the years depends on the hardware that carries it. From tracks and poles to holdbacks and tassels, we choose the accessories together with the fabric and fit them with the same care. The tracks, motors and controls of motorised systems belong to this family too.

Tracks and poles

Curtain tracks are fixed to the ceiling or into a curtain recess in a suspended ceiling; they let the curtain glide lightly and keep the carrier in the background. A rustic pole, by contrast, stays in view: a classic carrier that becomes part of the decoration with its finials. Which suits depends on the structure of your ceiling and the weight of the fabric, and we decide it together.

Holdbacks, tassels and small details

Holdbacks and tassels keep a curtain open during the day and shape the way the fabric falls. We choose them with you to match the tones of the pole and the fabric. At our office you can see the sample books and samples side by side before you decide.

Fitting and guarantee

Our own team fits tracks, poles and motorised systems. The right carrier is the first condition for a curtain that runs smoothly for years, so we plan the hardware on the same measuring visit as the curtain. Like every product we make, our accessories carry a 3-year guarantee.

Which track goes into a curtain recess in a suspended ceiling?

Ceiling tracks hidden in the recess give the cleanest result: the carrier is out of sight and the curtain seems to fall from the ceiling. The width of the recess decides whether one or two tracks fit, for a sheer and a drape; we look at this together when we measure.

Can you make a new curtain for my existing track?

Yes. When we come to measure we check your track and whether it can carry the weight of the curtain. If it can, we sew your new curtain to suit it.

Which track is right for heavy drapes?

Heavy, long drapes need a track strong enough to carry the load and enough fixing points. We weigh up the fabric and the drop of the curtain together when we measure, and choose the track to match.

What is a pelmet for?

A pelmet is a decorative profile fixed in front of the track and the top of the curtain. It hides the track and leaves one clean line above the window, which makes it a good way to conceal the track in rooms without a curtain recess in the ceiling. We choose its colour and profile together, to suit the room.

Where does a holdback go?

We fix the holdback to the wall beside the window, at the height where the curtain is to be gathered. We settle that height with you when we measure, going by the length of the curtain and the way the fabric falls, and we choose its colour to go with the pole or the metals in the room.

Do you fit the accessories yourselves?

Yes. Our own team fits tracks, poles and motorised systems, and our care support goes on after fitting.
