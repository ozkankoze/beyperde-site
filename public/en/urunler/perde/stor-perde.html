One piece, one clean line

A roller blind is a single length of fabric that winds onto a tube above the window. Open, it almost disappears; closed, it makes a flat, plain surface in front of the glass. A zebra blind is a version of the same system in which sheer and opaque bands slide over one another; by lining the bands up you adjust the light step by step.

Fabric choices

The fabric gives a roller blind its character. Light-filtering fabrics soften the daylight while keeping a link with the outside; blackout fabrics shut the light out in bedrooms and for hours in front of a screen. A zebra blind brings the two together in living rooms and studies where you want to adjust the light often. You choose the fabric from our sample books, in your own light.

Where it works

Roller blinds do well in offices, kitchens, children's rooms and any room meant to look plain. They work on their own, or behind a drape as a second layer that manages the light. On wide fronts and windows that are hard to reach we fit roller blinds as motorised systems, with the motor hidden inside the tube the fabric winds onto.

The details

A roller blind shows more than its fabric: the bottom rail, the chain and the box that hides the mechanism. We choose their colours with you, to suit the fabric and the room. If you like, we set the mechanism inside a box, so that from the room only the fabric and the bottom rail show.

Measuring, fitting and guarantee

A roller blind can be fitted inside the window recess or above the window; we decide which suits together when we measure. It is made to your size, orders are ready within 3–8 working days, and our own team fits it. Like every product we make, our roller blinds carry a 3-year guarantee.

What is the difference between a roller and a zebra blind?

A roller blind is a single piece of fabric, used open or closed. In a zebra blind, sheer and opaque bands slide over each other; by lining them up you can adjust the light gradually without raising the blind.

Will a blackout roller blind make the room completely dark?

Blackout fabric stops the light passing through the cloth. To reduce light leaking round the edges we recommend fitting the blind above the window, wider than the recess; we settle the right way when we measure, after looking at your window.

Can a roller blind be motorised?

Yes. In a motorised roller blind the motor sits inside the tube the fabric winds onto, out of sight. You can run the blind by remote or from your phone, and open and close several windows at once.

Does a wide window take a single roller blind?

First we look at the window's width and how it is divided. On wide glass we can also fit the blind as several pieces side by side, following the window's sections; each piece opens and closes on its own, so you can close off one part of the room and leave another open. We decide together which suits when we measure.

Can a roller blind be used with a drape?

Yes. The roller blind becomes the layer that manages the light at the window, while the drape at either side carries the room's texture and warmth. When we plan both on one window, we settle with you, when we measure, whether the blind goes inside or above the window, and where the drape's track runs.

How do I clean a roller blind?

Dusting the fabric with a soft brush, or a vacuum cleaner on low suction, is enough. Clean marks with a slightly damp cloth, without rubbing. If your fabric needs special care, our care support goes on after delivery, so just get in touch.
