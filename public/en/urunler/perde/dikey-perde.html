Light in vertical lines

A vertical blind is made of vertical fabric vanes hanging from a track at the top. The vanes turn on their own axes to set the light, and the blind stacks to the side to clear the glass. It gives wide façades, sliding doors and offices an orderly, quiet look.

How it works

Turn the vanes to adjust the light step by step, keeping your view out while the room stays hidden from passers-by. To open the blind fully, the vanes stack to one side or part from the middle to both sides. That makes it a practical choice for sliding doors and busy passages.

Where it is used

Vertical blinds come into their own at tall, wide windows, in winter gardens, offices and meeting rooms; the vertical line of the vanes makes a ceiling look higher. With light-filtering or blackout fabrics, they are suited to how the room is used.

Chain and cord

The bead chain turns all the vanes at once; the cord stacks the blind to the side or draws it closed again. A fine chain along the bottom links the vanes: the gaps stay even, and the vanes do not swing when the window is open.

Measuring, fitting and guarantee

A vertical blind is made to measure, and we take the measurements in your space ourselves. Orders are ready within 3–8 working days, and our own team fits them. Like all our products, our vertical blinds carry a 3-year guarantee.

What is the difference between vertical and Venetian blinds?

A Venetian blind has horizontal slats and draws up; a vertical blind has vertical vanes and stacks to the side. On wide, tall glazing and sliding doors the vertical blind is more practical, while at standard windows the Venetian blind is more common.

Is a vertical blind practical on a sliding door?

Yes. Stacked to the side, the vanes leave the doorway completely clear. We choose together which side the blind stacks to, by the way the door opens, so the stacked vanes never stand in the way.

Can a very wide window take a single vertical blind?

The track is made to measure, so a wide window can be covered by a single vertical blind. On very wide façades, splitting the blind into two or more parts makes it easier to use; we decide together which suits better when we measure.

Do vertical blinds black out a room?

With vanes in blackout fabric, most of the light is stopped when the vanes are closed. A little light can still come through where the vanes meet; for bedrooms that need full darkness, a roller blind or drapes may suit better.

What fabrics are vertical blinds made in?

The vanes come in light-filtering, semi-filtering and blackout fabrics. We choose the fabric with you from our sample books, according to the room's light and use.

How are vertical blinds cleaned?

Dusting the vanes regularly with a soft brush or a vacuum cleaner on low suction is enough. Marks can be wiped with a slightly damp cloth; the vanes unhook one by one, so they can be cleaned separately when needed.
