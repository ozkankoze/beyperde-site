The plain texture of nature

A sisal rug is woven from sisal, a fibre drawn from the leaves of the agave plant. Its matt surface, clear weave and earthy tones give a room a natural, quiet floor. Its strong fibres make it last, and it is warm underfoot.

Rooms it suits

Sisal does well in living rooms, studies, corridors and busy places such as entrance halls. Its plain texture sits as easily with modern furniture as with classic, and looks especially good on wooden and stone floors. As the fibre is sensitive to moisture, we do not recommend it for bathrooms, kitchens or outdoors.

Care

Caring for a sisal rug starts with regular vacuuming; the fibres hold dust on the surface, so it cleans easily. A spill should be blotted at once with a dry cloth, as sisal absorbs water and can mark when cleaned wet. For deep cleaning we recommend dry-cleaning methods.

Size, binding and guarantee

We cut your sisal rug to the size of your room and finish its edges with a binding, choosing the binding colour together with the rug and the room. We take the measurements in your room ourselves, and we work across Çekmeköy, Şile, Ağva, Riva and Beykoz. Like every product we make, our sisal rugs carry a 3-year guarantee.

Is a sisal rug water-resistant?

Sisal is a natural fibre, so it absorbs water; when it gets wet its colour can lighten or a mark can stay. That is why we do not recommend it for bathrooms, kitchens or outdoors, and spills should be blotted at once with a dry cloth.

How do I clean a sisal rug?

Regular vacuuming is enough; sisal fibres hold dust on the surface, so it cleans easily. For stains we recommend dry cleaning rather than wet methods.

Is sisal hard underfoot?

Sisal has a firmer, more textured surface than a wool rug; it is comfortable to walk on and warm underfoot. Where people sit on the floor, as in children's rooms, a softer texture may be the better choice.

Can a sisal rug be made to the size of my room?

Yes. We cut your sisal rug to the size of your room and finish its edges with a binding in the colour you choose. We take the measurements in your room ourselves.
