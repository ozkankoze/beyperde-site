To the room's own measure

How we measure

We take the measurements in your room, seeing where the furniture stands and how the doors open. Together we decide how close the rug comes to the walls and how far it runs under the sofas and the table. If it has to fit a recessed wall or a special place such as a stair landing, we look at that when we measure too.

The bound edge

The cut edges of the rug are closed with a binding that stops the fibres fraying and gives the rug a finished look. A binding close to the rug's own tone makes a quiet frame; one taken from another colour in the room makes a clear edge. We look at the samples together at our office or when we come to measure.

Material and guarantee

We choose the material and texture with you according to how the room is used: a busy corridor does not need what a bedroom needs. Coming from the same hands as your curtains and wallpaper, the floor's colour and texture stay in tune with the room's other surfaces. We work across Çekmeköy, Şile, Ağva, Riva and Beykoz, and like every product we make, our made-to-measure rugs carry a 3-year guarantee.

Who measures for a made-to-measure rug?

We take the measurements in your room ourselves. Looking at where the furniture stands, how the doors open and the margin the rug leaves at the walls, we settle the right size together.

What is a bound edge, and why does a rug need one?

If the edge of a cut rug is left open, its fibres can come loose over time. A binding closes that edge, keeps the rug's shape and gives it a finished look; you can choose its colour to suit the rug or the room.

Will the thickness of the rug stop a door from opening?

A thick rug can catch on doors with little clearance underneath. When we measure we check how high the doors sit above the floor, and plan the rug's thickness and placing accordingly.

Can a rug be made in shapes other than a rectangle?

We look together, when we measure, at areas that have to fit recesses, columns or a stair landing. We tell you on the spot what can be done, according to the material of the rug you choose.
