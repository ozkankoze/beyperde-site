For the walls that are lived with

A vinyl wallpaper is a wallcovering whose face is coated with a thin layer of vinyl, so that it can be wiped clean. As it stands up to stains, damp and wear, it is a safe choice for the busiest walls of a home. The range runs from embossed textures to flat finishes, and from plain colours to patterns.

Where it works

Vinyl wallpaper comes into its own in kitchens, corridors, stairwells, children's rooms and living rooms that are seldom without guests. In offices, hotels and shops it keeps the wall looking new for a long time. It can go in bathrooms too, though around the shower and the bath, where water hits the wall directly, we recommend another finish.

Cleaning and durability

A vinyl face is cleaned by wiping it with a damp cloth, and most marks do not leave the lasting stain they would on fabric or paper. It also stands up to knocks and rubbing better than paper wallpapers. As washability varies from product to product, we read the care symbols on each roll's label together when you choose.

Measuring, hanging and guarantee

We measure in your room, look at the state of the wall and work out the number of rolls, allowing for the pattern repeat. Our own team hangs the paper; if old wallpaper has to come off or the wall needs preparing, we talk it through beforehand. We work across Çekmeköy, Şile, Ağva, Riva and Beykoz, and like every product we make, our vinyl wallpapers carry a 3-year guarantee.

Can vinyl wallpaper be wiped clean?

Yes. A vinyl face can be wiped with a damp cloth, and some types are tough enough to be washed gently with a soft sponge. Avoid abrasive cleaners and hard brushes.

Can vinyl wallpaper go in a kitchen or bathroom?

Yes, its damp-resistant face means it can be used in kitchens and bathrooms. Around the shower and the bath, where water hits the wall directly, we recommend another finish rather than wallpaper.

Is vinyl wallpaper a good choice for a child's room?

Being wipeable and hard-wearing, it is a good choice for children's rooms. Pencil marks and fingerprints usually come off with a damp cloth.

Can vinyl wallpaper go over old wallpaper?

We do not recommend it. The cleanest and longest-lasting result comes once the old paper is stripped and the wall made smooth and dry. We look at your wall when we measure and tell you what it needs before hanging.
