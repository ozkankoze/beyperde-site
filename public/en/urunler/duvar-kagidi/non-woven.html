Easy to hang, clean to remove

Paste the wall, hang it dry

With non-woven wallpaper the paste goes on the wall, not on the paper, and the paper is laid on dry. There is no soaking time and its size does not change, so hanging is quicker and the seams are neater. It also shapes easily around awkward places such as ceiling corners and the edges of windows and doors.

A breathable backing

A non-woven backing lets air and moisture pass to some degree, which helps the wall to breathe. That makes it a comfortable choice for living spaces such as bedrooms and living rooms. Non-woven papers with a vinyl face put washability first instead; we choose between the two according to how the room is used.

Measuring, hanging and guarantee

When the time comes for a change, a non-woven paper usually comes off dry and in one piece, without tiring the wall and leaving it ready for the next paper. We measure in your room, work out the quantity and hang it with our own team. We work across Çekmeköy, Şile, Ağva, Riva and Beykoz, and like every product we make, our non-woven wallpapers carry a 3-year guarantee.

What is non-woven wallpaper?

It is wallpaper made on a backing of cellulose and synthetic fibres joined without weaving. As it neither stretches nor shrinks when pasted, it is easy to hang, keeps its seams closed and does not damage the wall when it is removed.

What is the difference between non-woven and vinyl wallpaper?

Non-woven describes the backing, vinyl the face; many vinyl wallpapers are made on a non-woven backing. Uncoated non-woven papers breathe better, while those with a vinyl face are easier to wipe clean.

Is non-woven wallpaper easy to remove?

Yes. Most non-woven wallpapers strip dry and in one piece, with no need to soak them, leaving the wall underneath ready for a new paper.

Can non-woven wallpaper be painted?

Some non-woven papers are made to be painted and can take any colour you like once they are hung. Not every paper is suitable for paint, so we settle this together when you choose, by looking at the product's specification.
