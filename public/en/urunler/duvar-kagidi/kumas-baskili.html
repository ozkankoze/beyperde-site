Your pattern, your measurements

A pattern of your own

With digital printing the design is not limited to a sample book: a motif, a colour gradient or a pattern that matches the room's other textiles can be laid over the fabric texture. To catch the same tone as your curtains, upholstery or rug, we choose the colours by comparing them with samples. We can talk through the pattern you have in mind when we come to measure.

Where it works

A fabric print is at its best on the bedhead wall of a bedroom, as a feature wall in a living room, in hotel rooms and in boutique shops. Because it treats a whole wall as one composition, its effect grows on large surfaces. Coming from the same team as your curtains and rugs, it keeps the room's three surfaces in tune.

Measuring, hanging and guarantee

Before printing we measure the wall in your room and note its windows, doors and corners, so the print is prepared to the wall's real size. Our own team hangs it, matching the pattern from panel to panel. We work across Çekmeköy, Şile, Ağva, Riva and Beykoz, and like every product we make, our fabric-print wallpapers carry a 3-year guarantee.

How is fabric-print wallpaper different from a textile wallcovering?

A textile wallcovering has a face of real fabric. On a fabric-print wallpaper the texture and pattern of the fabric are digitally printed, which gives full freedom of pattern and size and usually makes it easier to look after.

Can I have a pattern of my own choice printed?

Yes, in digital printing the pattern and colour are prepared for you. We look at the design you have in mind together with the size of the room and your other textiles, and settle the pattern and measurements with you before it goes to print.

Can fabric-print wallpaper be wiped clean?

If the base it is printed on has a wipeable face, marks can be cleaned with a damp cloth. A bedroom and a corridor ask different things of a wall, so we choose the base according to how the room is used.

Which rooms suit fabric-print wallpaper?

It is used most on bedhead walls, as a feature wall in living rooms, in hotel rooms and in boutique shops. With a wipeable base it can also go in busier places such as corridors and dining rooms.
